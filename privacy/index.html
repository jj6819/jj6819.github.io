Privacy Policy

Overview

NightOwl Sleep Calc is a free sleep calculator. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum. This policy explains how information is handled on our website.

What We Collect

Contact Form: If you contact us, we receive your name, email, and message through Formspree, a third-party form service.

Local Storage: Your calculator settings (like sleep latency and cycle length) are saved in your browser. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising & Cookies

We display ads through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show you relevant ads based on your browsing activity. You can opt out at Google's Ads Settings or learn more at Google's Privacy & Terms.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect ad display.

Third-Party Services

We use Formspree for contact forms and Google AdSense for advertising. These services have their own privacy policies.
