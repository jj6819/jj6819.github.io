Sleep Cycle Calculator

A sleep cycle calculator helps you plan bedtimes and wake-up times by counting full sleep cycles. NightOwl starts with a typical 90-minute cycle and lets you adjust the timing so you can find options that feel more natural and less groggy.

What is a sleep cycle?

Sleep cycles repeat through the night as your brain moves through lighter sleep, deeper sleep, and REM. A full cycle often averages about 90 minutes, but it can vary by person and by night.

Why waking at cycle boundaries can help

Waking from deep sleep often triggers sleep inertia—the "foggy" feeling. Planning to wake closer to the end of a cycle can reduce the odds of waking at the worst moment.

How NightOwl calculates times

NightOwl takes your target time and works backward or forward using:

  • Cycle length (default 90 minutes)
  • Sleep latency (time to fall asleep)
  • Wake window (a flexible range)

Best cycle counts (what most people choose)

Many adults feel best with about 5–6 cycles (roughly 7.5–9 hours of sleep time, plus latency). If you're short on time, 4 cycles can be a workable minimum.

FAQ

Why 90 minutes?

It's a common average. If your mornings feel consistently early/late, adjust cycle length slightly.

How accurate is cycle timing?

It's a useful guide, not a guarantee—sleep stages aren't perfectly predictable.

What if I take a long time to fall asleep?

Increase sleep latency so bedtime suggestions match reality.
