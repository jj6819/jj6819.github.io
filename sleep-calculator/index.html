Sleep Calculator

NightOwl is a free sleep calculator that helps you plan the best time to go to bed or wake up—without obsessing over a single exact minute. It uses sleep cycles, sleep latency (how long you take to fall asleep), and a wake window (a flexible range around the recommended time) so the results are easier to follow in real life.

What this sleep calculator does

This tool gives you two quick ways to plan:

  • Wake up at… → get recommended bedtimes based on full cycles.
  • Bedtime now → get recommended wake-up windows starting from the current time.

You can also use the nap calculator to choose a short reset nap or a full-cycle nap.

Why cycle timing can feel better than "hours only"

"8 hours" is a helpful target, but how you feel can depend on when you wake. Waking from deeper sleep can cause sleep inertia (grogginess). Planning around cycles helps you aim for a lighter phase near the end of a cycle.

How to use NightOwl (quick steps)

  1. Choose Wake up at… or Bedtime now
  2. Set Sleep Latency to match your routine (often 10–20 minutes)
  3. Keep Cycle Length at 90 minutes, or adjust if needed
  4. Set your Wake Window for flexibility
  5. Pick an option that fits your schedule and try it for a few mornings

What to adjust if results feel "off"

If you keep waking up too early or too late compared to how you feel, adjust cycle length by 5–10 minutes. If bedtime suggestions don't match your real nights, increase sleep latency.

FAQ

How much sleep do adults need?

Most adults do best around 7–9 hours, but your ideal can vary with sleep debt, activity, stress, and schedule.

Is this medical advice?

No—this is an educational planning tool. If you have persistent sleep issues, consider a clinician.

What's the wake window?

A small range around a recommended time so you can pick what's realistic without losing the overall cycle timing.
