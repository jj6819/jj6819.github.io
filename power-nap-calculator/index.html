Nap Calculator

Naps can be a quick way to reset your energy—if the timing is right. NightOwl's nap calculator helps you pick a nap length and shows a wake window so you can wake at a better moment.

Common nap types (and what they're for)

  • Coffee nap (15 minutes): drink coffee, nap briefly, wake as caffeine kicks in
  • Power nap (20 minutes): quick boost with minimal grogginess for many people
  • Full cycle nap (90 minutes): longer nap that can include a full sleep cycle

Why naps can make you groggy

Grogginess often happens when you wake from deeper sleep. Short naps can reduce that risk; longer naps can work better if you allow time for a full cycle.

How to use the nap calculator

Pick a nap type, then aim for the suggested wake window. If you have an important meeting, choose the shortest option and give yourself a few minutes to fully wake up.

FAQ

Are naps bad for nighttime sleep?

They can be if they're late or long—try earlier in the day and keep them short if bedtime is affected.

What's the best nap length?

Many people do well with 15–20 minutes; 90 minutes can work if you have time.

Why does the nap calculator use a wake window?

A range is easier to follow and reduces "perfect timing" stress.
